## Company
A leader in self-serve research and insights tooling, Attest sets itself apart from the competition by pairing state-of-the-art technology with a team of caring and knowledgeable research and insights professionals.
Attest's key aim is to make research effortless and minimise the time from question to answer from counting in days/weeks, down to minutes/hours.

## Stack
When I joined Attest in 2023 the company had an eclectic mix of application principles, languages, and styles.

A good portion of my time over the years here was dedicated to architectural simplification, initially to help engineers navigate the codebase and write a cohesive platform, but increasingly also to enable AI coding solutions to more effectively understand the platform as a whole, and write consistent applications that any engineer could parse and understand.

The platform today consists of Spring Boot Java Applications as the main surface, with core proprietary libraries that enable engineers to focus on problem solving and not application security and wiring. This sits in a Service-oriented architecture, with the vision always being a small, agile stack of services that are composable and extensible. On top of Java/Spring Boot applications the stack is rounded out by a handful of Python applications, these primarily serve as AI/ML services as the pace of innovation in this space is generally higher than in the Java world, at least for the time being.
Postgres forms the vast majority of the storage, with Kafka in place for async flows and Kubernetes in AWS as the Infra/Orchestration layer.

A key callout for the stack at Attest is the Contract-driven approach: all backend handler code (and much of the frontend API client code) is generated from contracts, not only enforcing security and endpoint standards, but building a contract-first organisation through coding practice.

## Role
My role at Attest has been a true pleasure, with no two days being the same. I have done everything from managing ICs directly, managers reporting to me, building entirely new products with a small agile squad, to regularly hosting the company's all-hands.

I have been a member of the Senior Leadership team throughout my time at Attest, as well as leading Product and Technology alongside the Director of Product and the CTPO, this has given me the ability to take a holistic approach to both team leadership and technical/architectural leadership.

Initially a lot of my day to day has been dedicated to creating small but significant change in the organisation through driving engineering efficiency, parts of this were pushing the risk averse team through aggressive architectural refactoring, this then shifted with the changes in the industry to consider not only how do we get the most out of our ICs, but then how do we deeply integrate AI into our engineering practice, product, and the company as a whole.

I was honoured to be given the opportunity to build and launch an entirely new product for Attest at the beginning of 2026, the first entirely new offering that Attest had put into the market since the start of the company 10 years prior. I led a very small team of engineers deep in agentic engineering, to build what would become Explore, an AI Moderated Interview platform, from a blank page to internal testing within 3 months, and into external beta before the first half of 2026 was over. Finishing out 2026 after handing off the fledgling product to a new team to continue to build and maintain, I then set my sights on how taking the learnings around pace and launching a second offering could shape our platform direction and agentic engineering practice. Factory Models, Platform 2.0, Security and Auditing all became key focuses when looking at how to build the next version of Attest.
