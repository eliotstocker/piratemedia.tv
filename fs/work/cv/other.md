## Profile

Over the past 15+ years, I have built companies, teams, and platforms of all sizes, from small three-man teams and startups, through to multi-team groups within scale-ups.

Initially coming from a background in music production, I bring a different perspective to software engineering from my non-traditional background. Many of my roles to date have focused on media, and developer experience, bringing in the valuable skills I gained working in the media and communications industries.

My passions are communication, culture, and efficiency. I believe that a message communicated the right way, to a team that has built trust can be so much louder.

My Core Tenets for Leadership:

1. Culture and trust come first, when people know they can talk to a leader about anything, the conversation opens up to find the optimal path quickly and happily.
2. Well planned, designed, and delivered communication is worth more than 1 million lines of code.
3. Efficiency means communicating more, not less.

Platform and Contracts are words that I'm sure many have tired of hearing from me, but I have a strong belief that building strong contracts between systems, as well as the teams building them, nurtures the most effective and efficient engineering organisations. Good documentation, APIs and extensibility are key to engineering efficiency and a cohesive internal and external platform.

Whilst my focus is squarely on leadership, I'm not at all afraid to get my hands dirty; you won't find me shying away from helping members of my teams solve complex problems.
