# Patterns

There are many patterns out there; however, I have found a constantly evolving toolbox including some of the following:

* Service-oriented architecture, with clear documentation for humans and agents
* Contract-first development, keeping all software accountable to what we said it would do
* Spec-driven "factory" engineering model, a rock-solid first pass at any well-formed idea with no human intervention
* Tried and tested technologies, sticking with what works, e.g. PostgreSQL (40 years old), Java (31 years old), Containerisation (18 to 47 years old depending on who you ask)
* Keeping pace with change, we don't have to jump on every shiny new thing, but we must be aware of it.
* Little and often communication, a small check in with the right people frequently is much more valuable than a huge meeting once a month.
